New to the numbers? A quick guide

Revenue is what the business sells. Operating margin shows the profit left from operations per dollar of sales. Free cash flow (FCF) is cash from operations after capital spending. FCF yield compares that cash to the price of the business’s equity. TTM means the latest four reported quarters. High growth can be valuable, but look at the cash and capital needed to achieve it.

How to read this comparison

Lime cells mark the leader for that metric within the selected group, including ties. There is no overall buy/sell score. Growth, margins, ROIC proxy, and FCF yield favor higher values; EV/EBITDA and net debt/EBITDA favor lower values. Negative net debt means net cash. Nonpositive earnings or EBITDA produce unavailable multiples. Different business mixes, fiscal periods, accounting policies, and leverage can limit comparability.

ROIC proxy = TTM operating income × (1 − assumed 21% tax) / average invested capital at the year-ago and latest available quarter. Invested capital = book equity + total debt − cash & short-term investments. Cash-rich and financial businesses can make this measure unhelpful. Banks, insurers, and REITs need sector-specific methods.

What the numbers cannot tell you

Read customer and supplier concentration, revenue recognition, related-party transactions, auditor opinions, internal controls, legal proceedings, debt covenants, guarantees, and purchase commitments. DCF method

This is a custom five-year unlevered free-cash-flow model, not a valuation supplied by a market-data vendor. It starts with the latest four aligned reported quarters of revenue. Growth, operating margin, and CapEx/revenue transition linearly between the model’s starting and year-5 settings.

FCF = EBIT × (1 − cash tax rate) + D&A − CapEx − Δ working capital. Taxes apply only to positive EBIT. D&A is modeled as a percentage of revenue; working capital as a percentage of incremental revenue.

Terminal value = Year-5 FCF × (1 + g) / (WACC − g). Each forecast cash flow and the terminal value are discounted to today. Per-share equity value = (enterprise value + cash & short-term investments − debt) / diluted shares.

WACC is an editable assumption; this app does not estimate it from beta, a risk-free rate, or a capital-structure model. Analyst price targets stay separate. The optional EPS-growth proxy changes initial revenue growth only; it is not a consensus revenue forecast.

Unlevered free cash flow = EBIT − cash taxes on positive EBIT + depreciation & amortization − capital expenditures − change in working capital. Growth, EBIT margin, and CapEx/revenue transition linearly across five years. Terminal cash flow grows from year 5 at the terminal growth rate and is discounted at WACC. Equity value adds cash and subtracts debt; a negative modeled equity value is floored at zero per share.

No future dilution, acquisitions, asset sales, tax-loss carryforwards, lease adjustments, or other claims are modeled. Terminal reinvestment remains implicit in year-5 CapEx, D&A, and working capital; review those assumptions for consistency. Defaults are a starting point, not a forecast. The method is generally unsuitable for financial institutions and REITs.

Read the three clocks

1. Source timestamp

When the quote traded or the article was published. A price from Friday can be a healthy feed on a weekend.

2. Financial or forecast period

The quarter/year the figure describes. A current retrieval can still contain an older reported quarter or analyst consensus period.

3. Retrieval or diagnostic time

When the app fetched a provider response or ran a test. This checks delivery, not whether the underlying figure is newly published.

Stale snapshots, source failures, and missing coverage are shown explicitly. Check the row’s context and the timestamps before using a figure in a pitch.

Coverage, storage & limitations

  • Financial comparisons and DCF defaults target USD-listed equities with USD financial statements.
  • Banks, insurers, REITs, foreign listings, recent IPOs, and thinly covered stocks may need different methods or manual inputs.
  • Estimated market value uses latest diluted average shares. ROIC is a labeled proxy. Neither should be mistaken for an official company-reported figure.
  • Watchlists, notes, saved models, and last-successful snapshots live in this browser. They are not a shared fund database and do not sync across devices.
  • Model results depend on your assumptions. Automated screens identify questions; they do not establish fraud, safety, or investment suitability.

Working with the model

Reported financial inputs initialize the DCF when coverage permits. Growth, tax, WACC, reinvestment, and terminal assumptions require judgment. Price updates follow the quote only when “Use latest quote in model” is selected. Your saved assumptions remain company-specific.

Analyst EPS growth can optionally be used as an explicitly labeled initial revenue-growth proxy. EPS growth and revenue growth are not equivalent. Review that shortcut before presenting it.
